How the tiers were derived — not invented

A points scorecard (month-to-month 3 · tenure ≤6m 3 / ≤12m 2 / ≤24m 1 · fiber 2 · no support 2 · e-check 2 · no security 1 · senior 1) produces a score of 0–14. The cut-offs sit where the observed churn rate steps up: score ≥8 is HIGH, 5–7 MEDIUM, ≤4 LOW. The scorecard lives in SQL (sql/01_data_exploration.sql), so the dashboard and the notebook cannot disagree about "high risk".

Definitions. Churn rate = churned ÷ customers (one row per customer). Retention = 1 − churn. MRR = Σ MonthlyCharges. Revenue at risk = Σ MonthlyCharges over churned customers × 12 — a run-rate exposure, not realised loss. Priority tiers come from the SQL risk scorecard (page 3).
